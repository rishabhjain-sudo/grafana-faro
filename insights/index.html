Product insights

Open-source product analytics on Grafana Faro events stored in Loki. Pick a view:

▶ Session replay

Watch real visits recorded with Faro's rrweb-based replay add-on. Filter abandoned carts, purchases and errors.

⏬ Funnels & drop-off

Strict, ordered funnels per session, with step conversion, time between steps, drop-off counts, exit pages and links to replays.

↻ Retention

Daily or weekly cohorts by first visit: what share comes back on day N.

⇢ User paths

Most common journeys, a flow diagram, and what happens before and after any page.

▦ Heatmaps

Click heatmaps drawn on the live page, per page and device, plus the most-clicked elements.

▤ Grafana dashboard ↗

KPIs, web vitals, errors and ad-hoc LogQL in Explore.
